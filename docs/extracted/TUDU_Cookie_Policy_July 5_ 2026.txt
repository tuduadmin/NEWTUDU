2. What Are Cookies?
Cookies are small text files placed on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device, remember your preferences and past actions, and collect information about how the site is used. Without cookies, a site would have no way of remembering that you were just there — every page you visit would treat you like a first-time stranger.
Cookies may be:
First-party cookies — set directly by TUDU.
Third-party cookies — set by services we work with, such as Google Maps.
Session cookies — temporary cookies deleted when you close your browser.
Persistent cookies — cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.
We may also use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and local storage, which work alongside cookies to collect information about your use of the Site. In this policy, we refer to all of these collectively as "cookies."
3. Why We Use Cookies
Cookies serve a few practical purposes for us and for you as a visitor:
Making the Site work — some cookies are required just to load pages correctly and keep the Site secure.
Remembering you — cookies let the Site recall choices you've already made, so you don't have to re-enter the same information every visit.
Understanding usage — cookies help us see which pages and features people actually use, so we can prioritize improvements that matter.
Supporting embedded features — some cookies come from third-party tools we embed directly into the Site, like maps and sign-in options.
We do not use cookies to sell your personal information to third parties.

6. How to Control Cookies
You have several options to manage or limit cookies:
Cookie banner: When available, our cookie consent tool lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies when you first visit the Site.
Browser settings: Most browsers let you block, limit, or delete cookies through their settings menu. Note that blocking essential cookies may affect the Site's functionality.
Third-party opt-outs: You can manage preferences directly with providers like Google through their own account and ad settings tools.
Instructions for managing cookies in common browsers:
Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies
Disabling cookies through your browser applies broadly across the sites you visit, not just TUDU, so review your browser's help documentation for full details.
