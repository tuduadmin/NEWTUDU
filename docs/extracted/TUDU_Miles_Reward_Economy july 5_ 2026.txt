TUDU Miles Reward Economy
Internal Reference Document
1. Overview
The TUDU Miles Economy is the rewards and incentive system that powers user engagement, host growth, and long-term loyalty across the TUDU platform. Designed to feel simple for users yet sustainable for the business, TUDU Miles turns every action — attending events, creating experiences, purchasing boosts, building communities — into meaningful progress inside the app.
At its core, the economy is built on two principles: earn Miles from activity and spending, and redeem Miles for valuable tools and experiences.
Users earn 100 Miles for every $1 spent on the platform, creating a familiar and predictable rewards structure. Hosts earn additional Miles for reaching event-creation milestones, while attendees collect Miles through participation, engagement, and referrals. This creates a powerful ecosystem where everyone benefits from being active on TUDU.
To maintain balance and prevent inflation, TUDU uses a controlled redemption model: 800 Miles = $1 in redeemable value. This represents a 12.5% return over time — rewarding enough to motivate users, yet stable enough to protect the TUDU economy as it scales.
TUDU Miles can be exchanged for high-value items such as travel passes, event boosts, tickets, merchandise, and partner perks, giving users a sense of progression and unlocking deeper levels of participation. Subscriptions, however, remain cash-based to ensure platform sustainability, though they still generate Miles as an added benefit.
The result is a closed-loop, gamified economic system where users are rewarded for contributing, exploring, and hosting — driving continuous activity on the platform while reinforcing TUDU's mission: get people out, connected, engaged, and doing more.

4. Mile Economy Limitations
Miles expire after 24 months. Soft expiry at 18 months, hard expiry at 24 months, with a reminder issued before expiration.
Open question: Will need to determine the need for an expiry on Miles.
Lower earn rate applies to non-paying users.
Open question: Are users able to accumulate Miles on the Explorer (freemium) tier?
Subscriptions generate Miles but cannot be purchased with Miles.
Miles cannot be redeemed for cash withdrawal.
Host milestone Miles are awarded once per milestone (e.g., hitting 10 pays out; the next reward is at 25, not 10 again).
Miles will be revoked if fraud is detected.
