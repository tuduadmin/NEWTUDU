TUDU Milestone Awards
Internal Reference Document
1. Overview
Physical and digital rewards are mailed to hosts when they reach major hosting milestones, accompanied by a personalized letter. The program is designed to recognize host commitment with escalating prestige — from a first digital badge at 10 events, up to a lifetime-recognition trophy at 5,000+ events.

Bronze, Silver, and Gold tiers are presented as plaques. Emerald and Diamond tiers are presented as crystal trophies. Hosts may also purchase or redeem Miles for a physical award at the Bronze, Silver, or Gold tiers ahead of reaching that milestone naturally.
3. Tiered Digital Badges
Digital badges are awarded alongside each tier and can be displayed across the platform, including:
Host profile
Event cards
Comments
Search results
Map pins
4. Physical Awards
Each host also receives a personalized award letter. Emerald Pathfinder and Diamond Horizon recipients receive a fully personalized, signed letter; earlier tiers receive a PDF congratulations letter.
