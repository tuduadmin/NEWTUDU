Emails & Communications
Internal Reference Document
1. Overview
This document outlines TUDU's email structure and naming conventions. Role-based inboxes are set up in advance to support growth, while personal employee emails are created as each new hire is onboarded.
2. Naming Convention
Employee email addresses follow the format: first initial + last name (e.g., apalm@tudu.ca).
If two employees share the same first initial and last name, a number is appended to differentiate the accounts (e.g., apalm2@tudu.ca).
3. Current Emails

5. Direction & Open Considerations
Direction note: Confirm whether support@tudu.ca should be scoped to privacy questions only, or expanded to general customer support as the user base grows — may be worth splitting into support@ (general) and privacy@ (privacy-specific) once volume increases.
Direction note: Decide on a retention/handoff process for personal employee emails (e.g., apalm@tudu.ca) if that employee leaves the company, to avoid losing continuity with external contacts.
Direction note: Consider whether info@tudu.ca and support@tudu.ca should route into a shared inbox / helpdesk tool (e.g., Google Workspace groups, Zendesk) rather than a single personal mailbox, once inquiry volume increases.
Direction note: Determine whether noreply@tudu.ca should ever accept replies for compliance purposes (e.g., unsubscribe confirmations), since some jurisdictions require a working reply-to address on marketing emails.
