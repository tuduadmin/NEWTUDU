# TUDU Foundation — Strategy & Direction

> Source: TUDU_Foundation July 5, 2026.docx

## Overview

The TUDU Foundation extends TUDU's mission — getting people out, connected, and doing more — to kids who lack access to those experiences. A branded tour bus wrapped in TUDU character designs travels Canada bringing kids on memorable adventures, documented by the TUDU Crew.

## Mission Statement

> [DRAFT] The TUDU Foundation exists to give underprivileged kids access to unforgettable adventures, connection, and community — proving that opportunity and excitement shouldn't depend on a family's income.

## How It Works

- Branded tour bus travels across Canada with curated kid adventures.
- TUDU Crew documents trips for TUDU channels.
- Tour content doubles as authentic brand marketing.

## Relationship to TUDU Entertainment Inc.

- Initially a program under TUDU Entertainment Inc. (not separate charity).
- Expected spin-off into registered nonprofit as it scales.

## Funding Model

1. TUDU Entertainment revenue/resources commitment.
2. Public donations via future in-app or website Donate button.

## The Bus & Brand Presence

- Character-wrapped bus as mobile brand asset.
- Phase 1: one bus nationally; long-term: multi-bus, multi-continent.

## TUDU Crew Involvement

- On-camera hosts and guides on Foundation tours.
- Video content production at each stop.
- Background checks and media releases required for work with children.

## Growth Roadmap

| Phase | Milestone |
|-------|-----------|
| Phase 1 | Single bus, national Canada tour |
| Phase 2 | Charitable registration / spin-off |
| Phase 3 | Additional buses (Canada / U.S.) |
| Phase 4 | Multi-continent expansion |
